US AutoDarts League v0.7.12 Swipe Test

1. Install v0.7.12 over the existing app.
2. Sign in and open Settings.
3. Confirm bottom of Settings says: APP v0.7.12 • DIRECT TOUCH SWIPE
4. Tap League Rules.
5. Swipe RIGHT horizontally across the middle of the rule list while your finger is still on the screen.
   Expected: return to Settings.
6. Swipe LEFT horizontally across Settings.
   Expected: return forward to League Rules.
7. Vertical scrolling should still work normally.

This build does not use react-native-gesture-handler for navigation.
The ScrollView that owns the Android touch stream observes the swipe directly.
